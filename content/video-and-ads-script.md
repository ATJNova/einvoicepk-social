## 4. HOW TO BUILD IT IN HEYGEN (step-by-step)

1. Go to **app.heygen.com** → New Video → Portrait (9:16).
2. Pick an **avatar** (search "business", "Pakistani", or use a presenter you like) — or choose **Voiceover only** if you'd rather show only the slides.
3. Paste the **scene script** (§2) into the script box, one scene per slide.
4. For the **voice**: EN → an energetic English voice; UR → HeyGen supports Urdu, pick an Urdu voice and paste the Urdu script.
5. Upload our **slide PNGs** (build/out/*.png) as backgrounds / B-roll per scene.
6. Add **on-screen text** from the tables, brand colors, and the **end card** (logo + WhatsApp + URL).
7. Add **background music** (upbeat) at ~15% volume.
8. **Render** → download MP4. Make a 30s, then duplicate and trim to 15s and 6s.
9. Send me the MP4s (or drop in the repo) → I'll schedule them as Reels in Buffer and we plug them into the Meta ads.
