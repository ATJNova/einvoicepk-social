## ✅ VERIFIED FBR FACTS (use these — they're real)

**It's the law now.** FBR digital invoicing is mandatory for sales-tax registered persons.
Invoices issued outside the digital system after a business's deadline are treated as **illegal**.

**Rollout deadlines (SRO 1852(I)/2025, by turnover):**
- 1 Sep 2025 — public companies, turnover > Rs 1 billion, importers
- 1 Oct 2025 — turnover Rs 100 million–1 billion (incl. individuals/AOPs > Rs 100m)
- 1 Nov 2025 — companies with turnover ≤ Rs 100 million
- 1 Dec 2025 — **all other registered persons**
→ Every category's deadline has now passed.

**Penalties (Section 25A, Sales Tax Act 1990):**
- **First default: Rs 500,000**
- Repeat defaults: **Rs 1,000,000 → Rs 2,000,000 → Rs 3,000,000**
- **Daily penalties** (~Rs 25,000+/day) until you comply
- Business **sealing/suspension**, **blacklisting from government tenders**, higher audit risk

**Enforcement so far (reported Nov 2025):**
- 45,000+ businesses integrated
- **Rs 2.3 billion** already collected in penalties
- 350+ businesses temporarily sealed · 195 blacklisted

**What a valid invoice needs:** FBR invoice number (IRN), **QR code**, and **FBR logo**,
submitted through **PRAL or a licensed integrator** — which is exactly what einvoicepk does.

> ⚠️ Honesty guardrail: the penalty/mandate applies to **sales-tax registered** businesses.
> Frame the fear angle to that audience. For everyone else, lead with speed + free-to-start.
