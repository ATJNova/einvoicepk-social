## 🗣️ Language — Desi (Urdu-English mix)
- Write the way Pakistanis actually talk: **Roman Urdu + English mixed**, natural code-switching.
- Keep in **English:** product/tech/legal terms — FBR, PRAL, IRN, QR, invoice, NTN, dashboard, URLs, numbers.
- Everyday/emotional words stay **Roman Urdu:** aap, kaarobar, jaldi, mehfooz, asaan, fikr, bharosa, etc.
- Pure-Urdu (Nastaliq) is used only for the premium "trust" pieces; the default social voice is the **Roman mix**.
